Today my son was born.

At home we call him Voi — _elephant_ in Vietnamese. My wife and I have always
loved them: strong, gentle, smart, and tender with their own. We wanted to wish
a little of that on him.

I won't dress this up. He's here, he's healthy, and I don't think I've ever been
happier. I'm writing it down so that years from now I can read it back and
remember exactly how today felt.

Welcome to this world, Voi 🐘.
