Every day there's a pile of small things I want to do later. Review John's PR in
thirty minutes. Reply to that message after lunch. Check the deploy before I log
off. None of them are hard. The problem is I'm in the middle of something else
when they show up, and setting a proper reminder means stopping what I'm doing.

On my Mac that's open Reminders, make a new one, type the title, find the date
field, pick a time, close it. On my phone it's about the same, plus unlocking it
and getting distracted by whatever else is on the screen. It's not a lot of steps,
but it's enough that I usually don't bother. I tell myself I'll remember. I don't.

So I made [Tock](/products/tock/). Press ⌃⌘R from any app, type "review John's PR
in 30m", hit Enter. That's it. Thirty minutes later it pops up, I do the thing,
I mark it done. The whole setup takes about five seconds and I'm back to what I was
doing before I've lost my train of thought.

You write the time however it comes out of your fingers. "In 30m", "in 30min" and
"in 30 minutes" all mean the same thing. "Take the tea bag out in 90s" works as a
quick timer. "Call mom tomorrow at 5", "team lunch this Friday at noon", "pay rent
on the 1st" all do what you'd expect. There's no syntax to learn, and it shows you
what it understood before you press Enter, so there's no guessing.
It lives in the menu bar, has no account, no sync, and doesn't touch the network.

It's most useful when you're juggling a few things at once and want to push one
of them off without losing it, or when a thought hits you mid-task and you just
need to get it out of your head. That's most of my day, so I use it constantly.

It's free and runs on macOS 13 or later. You can grab it on
[the Tock page](/products/tock/).
